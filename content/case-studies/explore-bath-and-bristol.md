## The brief

Bath and Bristol are full of interesting places, but the best of them are not always the easiest to find. Explore Bath & Bristol is an emerging online resource for people who want to get to know the area: its hidden gems, its local businesses and creatives, and the events and communities that bring people together.

It needed a website that could grow into a proper local guide, covering places to visit, networking, events and tours, and that could welcome contributions from the people who make the area special.

:::

::: white

## What the project involved

The site was designed and built from scratch as a custom WordPress theme, built around the content.

- A places directory, with each place on its own page and sorted into categories such as pubs, museums, markets, food, history, music, walks and unusual finds
- Separate listings for Bath and Bristol, so visitors can browse by area
- A People section for featuring local makers, businesses and creatives
- An events calendar, including networking events with practical workshops
- A networking guide that gathers local groups, chambers, business networks and events in one place
- Informal tours of Bath and Bristol, with information in more than one language
- A blog for articles, local stories and unusual facts about the area
- A Get involved page, so businesses, writers, photographers and tour guides can ask to be featured
- User accounts, with a privacy policy and a page for people to manage their data rights under GDPR

:::

::: lime

### Evolution over time

Places, people, events and articles each have their own content type, so new entries can be added without touching any code. As the project evolves the site is set up to easily scale and adapt to the emerging needs.

:::

::: standard

## Built to be found

A local guide only works if people can find it. Every place has its own page with a clear title and description, categories group related places together, and the blog and event content give search engines plenty of local, specific topics to match against what people are searching for.

:::

::: white

## The result

A flexible, scalable online guide to the area, live at [bath2bristol.co.uk](https://www.bath2bristol.co.uk/).
