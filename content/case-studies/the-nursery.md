## The brief

The Nursery is a research agency that connects people, brands and creativity. They needed a distinctive identity and a website that could do more than present information: it also had to handle event sign-ups and keep track of who is attending.

:::

::: white

## What we did

- Developed the brand and designed the logo, a sprouting leaf that reflects the idea of growing ideas
- Chose a bold, colourful palette to carry across the website and future collateral
- Designed the data architecture behind the site, so events, sign-ups and attendees are all connected
- Built a website where visitors can sign up for events and the team can see and manage attendance

:::

::: lime

### More than a brochure site

Sign-ups are stored and linked to each event, so The Nursery can see who has registered and keep track of attendees without juggling spreadsheets.

:::

::: standard

## The result

A confident, recognisable brand and a site that works as a practical event tool, live at [thenursery.greencrown.studio](https://thenursery.greencrown.studio/).
