## The brief

Cocomarshmallow Books is a series of gentle picture books about big feelings, written and illustrated by Lysha Goode in Bath. Each story follows Coco the cat through one emotion at a time and comes with a mindfulness activity inside.

The books needed a home online where people could buy them directly. They also needed a clear plan for who to sell to and how to reach them, because a good shop is only useful if the right people find it.

:::

::: white

## What we did

- Helped shape a sales strategy, working out who the books are for and where the best opportunities lie
- Built the ecommerce website, with a shop, product pages, postage information and a straightforward checkout
- Worked on the online proposal for nurseries and preschools, so the offer is clear and easy to say yes to
- Created a Resources for nurseries section that explains how the books support early emotional intelligence in the classroom
- Set up downloadable activities and colouring sheets, free to use alongside each book
- Thought about SEO from the start, so the site can be found by parents, grandparents, teachers and nursery practitioners searching for the things they need

:::

::: lime

### More than a website

The site is part of the sales strategy, not separate from it. The free printables bring people in, the nursery resources show practitioners why the books belong in their setting, and the shop is there when they are ready to buy.

:::

::: standard

## Built to be found

Different people look for these books in different ways. A parent might search for a picture book about feelings. A nursery practitioner might search for resources to help children talk about emotions. The site has pages written for each of them, with clear headings, descriptive page titles and useful content that answers the question they came with.

Free printable activities and colouring sheets also give people a reason to visit, share the site and come back.

:::

::: white

## The result

A professional online shop and a joined-up plan for selling the books, with resources that give nurseries and families something useful before they spend anything. It is live at [cocomarshmallow.co.uk](https://cocomarshmallow.co.uk/).
