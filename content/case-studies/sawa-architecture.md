## The brief

SAWA Architecture came to us as a growing architecture practice with no existing brand — no logo, no colour palette, no website — needing to look established from day one.

:::

::: white

## What we did

- Created the initial brand identity and logo from scratch
- Chose a colour scheme to carry across the site and future collateral
- Built a bespoke WordPress website using Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) and flexible content

:::

::: lime

### Built so he can run it himself

The site was structured around ACF's flexible content so SAWA can add new project pages and reorganise the portfolio without ever needing a developer — content updates are his to make, whenever he needs to.

:::

::: standard

## The result

A complete visual identity and a self-manageable website, live at [sawa-architecture.org](https://www.sawa-architecture.org/).
