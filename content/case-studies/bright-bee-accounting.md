## The brief

Bright Bee Accounting is a virtual accountancy for small limited companies and sole traders across the UK. Her existing website was built on the Ionos site builder, and she was struggling to get it looking and working the way she wanted. She needed a site that felt professional, explained her services clearly and was easy for clients to find.

:::

::: white

## What we did

- Built a brand new, highly secure static website
- Made design and technical changes to the existing Ionos site she was struggling with, so it worked properly while the new one was built
- Developed her branding and the clarity of the website, saving her the cost of a separate branding project
- Carried out technical SEO changes so search engines can understand and rank the site
- Helped her arrange and shape her content, including a clear packages section that explains each service level

:::

::: lime

### Secure by design

A static site has no database or admin login to attack, which makes it very hard to compromise. It's also fast to load and needs very little ongoing maintenance.

:::

A confident, clear website with a strong bee-themed brand identity, live at [brightbeeaccounting.co.uk](https://www.brightbeeaccounting.co.uk/).
