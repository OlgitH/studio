## The brief

Marnie Kavanagh is a relational psychotherapist based in Bath. She needed a warm, calm online presence that reflected how she works, a way for prospective clients to get in touch easily, and a site she could look after herself.

:::

::: white

## What we did

- Designed the Marnie Therapy logo, a golden butterfly paired with a classic serif wordmark
- Built a custom website adapted to the way her practice runs, with pages for therapy, about, FAQs, contact and a blog
- Set up the contact form so website enquiries land directly in her email inbox

:::

::: lime

### Built so she can run it herself

Every element of the site is editable by Marnie, from the wording and images to the blog, without needing a developer. She can keep her information up to date as her practice grows.

:::

::: standard

## The result

A gentle, trustworthy website and brand identity that she controls completely, live at [marnietherapy.co.uk](https://marnietherapy.co.uk).

:::
